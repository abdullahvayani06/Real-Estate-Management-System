"# REMS_2" 
Business Requirements: The primary objective of the Real Estate Management System (REMS V2) is to provide a centralized, scalable, and automated platform for real estate brokerages. Operating on an Agency/Brokerage model, the system streamlines property inventory, client directories, and commission tracking. The high-level business goal is to deploy this software as a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) product, generating revenue by renting it to local real estate agencies on an annual subscription basis.
Problem & Challenge: Local real estate agencies frequently struggle with fragmented data management. Relying on manual ledgers or basic spreadsheets leads to data redundancy, lost client leads, and inaccurate calculation of agency commissions, ultimately slowing down the deal-closure process.
Limitations of the Current System: Currently, the system has two primary constraints. First, a mobile-responsive version has not yet been designed, limiting accessibility for field agents. Second, the financial module only records full, single-payment transactions for closed deals; it currently lacks the architecture to track partial payments or installment-based financial schedules.
